COMPREHENSIVE DUAS — 50+ AUTHENTIC SUPPLICATIONS
Source: Quran, Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Hisn al-Muslim

SECTION 1: MORNING AND EVENING ADHKAR

DUA 1: Morning Remembrance
Arabic: أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ
Transliteration: Asbahna wa asbahal-mulku lillahi wal-hamdu lillahi la ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah
Translation: We entered the morning and the whole kingdom belongs to Allah. All praise is due to Allah. None has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone without partner.
Reference: Abu Dawud 5077

DUA 2: Sayyid al-Istighfar (Master of Seeking Forgiveness)
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Transliteration: Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta, khalaqtani wa ana abduka, wa ana ala ahdika wa wadika mastatattu, audhu bika min sharri ma sanatu, abuu laka bi nimatika alayya, wa abuu laka bi dhanbi, faghfir li fa innahu la yaghfirudh-dhunuba illa anta
Translation: O Allah, You are my Lord. None has the right to be worshipped but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I hold to Your covenant and promise as best I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil I have done. I acknowledge Your blessings upon me and my sins. Forgive me, for none forgives sins but You.
Reference: Sahih Bukhari 6306
Virtue: Whoever says this during the day with firm belief and dies that day, he is among the people of Paradise.

DUA 3: Morning/Evening Protection
Arabic: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Transliteration: Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru maasmihi shayun fil-ardi wa la fis-samai wa huwas-Samiul-Alim
Translation: In the Name of Allah with Whose Name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, He is the All-Hearing, All-Knowing.
Reference: Abu Dawud 5088
Virtue: Whoever says this 3 times, nothing will harm him.

SECTION 2: PRAYER-RELATED DUAS

DUA 4: After Tashahhud Before Salam
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ وَمِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ وَمِنْ شَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ
Translation: O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of Hellfire, the punishment of the grave, the trial of living and dying, and from the evil of the trial of the False Messiah.
Reference: Sahih Muslim 588

DUA 5: After Salam
Arabic: أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ (٣) اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
Translation: I seek Allah's forgiveness (3x). O Allah, You are Peace, from You comes peace. Blessed are You, Owner of majesty and honour.
Reference: Sahih Muslim 591

DUA 6: Tasbeeh After Prayer
SubhanAllah (33x), Alhamdulillah (33x), Allahu Akbar (33x), then La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lahu lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa ala kulli shayin qadir.
Reference: Sahih Muslim 597
Virtue: Sins forgiven even if like the foam of the sea.

SECTION 3: DAILY LIFE DUAS

DUA 8: After Eating — Alhamdulillahil-ladhi atamani hadha wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah (Abu Dawud 4023). Virtue: Past sins forgiven.
DUA 9: Before Sleeping — Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya (Sahih Bukhari 6312)
DUA 10: Upon Waking — Alhamdulillahil-ladhi ahyana bada ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur (Sahih Bukhari 6312)
DUA 11: Leaving Home — Bismillahi tawakkaltu alallahi wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah (Abu Dawud 5095). Virtue: You will be guided, sufficed, and protected.
DUA 12: Entering Mosque — Allahummaftah li abwaba rahmatik (Sahih Muslim 713)
DUA 13: Leaving Mosque — Allahumma inni asaluka min fadlik (Sahih Muslim 713)
DUA 14: Entering Bathroom — Allahumma inni audhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khabaith (Sahih Bukhari 142)
DUA 15: Looking in Mirror — Allahumma anta hassanta khalqi fa hassin khuluqi (Musnad Ahmad 3823)
DUA 16: Wearing New Clothes — Allahumma lakal-hamdu anta kasawtanihi (Abu Dawud 4020)

SECTION 4: TRAVEL

DUA 17: Starting a Journey
Arabic: سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ
Translation: Glory be to Him who subjected this to us, we could not have done it ourselves. To our Lord we shall return.
Reference: Quran 43:13-14, Abu Dawud 2602

DUA 18: Returning from Travel — Ayibuna taibuna abiduna li Rabbina hamidun (Sahih Bukhari 1797)

SECTION 5: DISTRESS AND HARDSHIP

DUA 19: Anxiety and Worry
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ
Translation: O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, helplessness and laziness, cowardice and stinginess, and from debt burden and being overpowered by men.
Reference: Sahih Bukhari 6363

DUA 20: In Times of Difficulty
Arabic: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ الْعَظِيمُ الْحَلِيمُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ
Translation: None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, the Great, the Forbearing. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, Lord of the Magnificent Throne.
Reference: Sahih Bukhari 6346

DUA 21: Dua of Yunus — In Desperation
Arabic: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
Translation: There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.
Reference: Quran 21:87
Virtue: No Muslim prays with these words except that Allah answers him (Tirmidhi 3505).

DUA 22: When in Debt
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ
Translation: O Allah, suffice me with what You have allowed instead of what You have forbidden, and make me independent of all others besides You.
Reference: Tirmidhi 3563

DUA 23: When Angry — Audhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim (Sahih Bukhari 6115)

DUA 24: For Healing
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ اشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا
Translation: O Allah, Lord of mankind, remove the ailment. Cure, for You are the One who cures. There is no cure except Your cure — a cure leaving no sickness behind.
Reference: Sahih Bukhari 5742

DUA 25: Visiting the Sick — La basa tahurun in shaAllah (Sahih Bukhari 5656)

SECTION 6: GUIDANCE AND FORGIVENESS

DUA 26: Istikharah (Full)
Arabic: اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ وَأَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ الْعَظِيمِ فَإِنَّكَ تَقْدِرُ وَلَا أَقْدِرُ وَتَعْلَمُ وَلَا أَعْلَمُ وَأَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ
Translation: O Allah, I seek Your guidance through Your knowledge, and I seek ability through Your power, and I ask You from Your immense favour. You are able and I am not. You know and I do not, and You are the Knower of the unseen...
Reference: Sahih Bukhari 1162
When: After 2 Rakat of Nafl prayer when seeking guidance for a decision.

DUA 27: For Forgiveness
Arabic: أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ
Translation: I seek forgiveness of Allah besides whom there is no god, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining, and I repent to Him.
Reference: Abu Dawud 1517

DUA 28: Rabbana from the Quran
Arabic: رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Translation: Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and save us from the punishment of the Fire.
Reference: Quran 2:201. The Prophet ﷺ used to make this dua more than any other.
