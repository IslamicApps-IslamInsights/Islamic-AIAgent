CHAPTER 1: THE SIX PILLARS OF IMAN
═══════════════════════════════

The Prophet ﷺ was asked about Iman by Angel Jibreel. He replied:
"Iman is to believe in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Last Day, and to believe in Divine Decree (Qadr) — both its good and its evil."
(Sahih Muslim 8 — Hadith of Jibreel)

CHAPTER 2: TAWHEED — ONENESS OF ALLAH
═══════════════════════════════

"Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent." (Quran 112:1–4)

2.1 Categories of Tawheed
---
1. Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah (Lordship):
   Allah alone is the Creator, Sustainer, and Controller of the universe.
   "Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is the Guardian over all things." (Quran 39:62)

2. Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah (Worship):
   Allah alone deserves all worship. No act of worship (dua, sacrifice, vows, seeking help) should be directed to other than Allah.
   "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help." (Quran 1:5)

3. Tawheed al-Asma' was-Sifat (Names and Attributes):
   Allah is described only by the names and attributes mentioned in the Quran and authentic Sunnah, without:
   - Tashbih (likening them to creation)
   - Ta'til (denying them)
   - Takyif (specifying their nature/how)
   - Tamthil (giving them human form)
   "There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing." (Quran 42:11)

2.2 Shirk (Polytheism) — The Greatest Sin
---
"Indeed, Allah does not forgive association with Him, but He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills." (Quran 4:48)

Types of Shirk:
1. Shirk al-Akbar (Major Shirk): Worshipping other than Allah, making Dua to the dead, believing others share Allah's attributes.
   This nullifies Islam entirely.
2. Shirk al-Asghar (Minor Shirk): Riya' (showing off in worship), swearing by other than Allah.
   The Prophet ﷺ said: "The thing I fear most for you is minor shirk — Riya'." (Musnad Ahmad 23630)

2.3 The Attributes of Allah
---
Allah has perfect attributes of:
- Life (Al-Hayy — الحي)
- Knowledge (Al-'Alim — العليم) — knows the seen and unseen
- Power (Al-Qadir — القادر) — over all things
- Will (Al-Murid) — whatever He wills comes to pass
- Hearing (As-Sami' — السميع)
- Sight (Al-Basir — البصير)
- Speech (Al-Mutakallim) — the Quran is the Speech of Allah
- Creation — He created everything from nothing

Where is Allah?
"The Most Merciful rose over the Throne." (Quran 20:5)
The Prophet ﷺ asked a bondwoman: "Where is Allah?" She said: "Above the heavens." He said: "Free her, for she is a believer." (Sahih Muslim 537)
The scholars of Ahlus-Sunnah affirm that Allah is above His creation, above the Throne, in a manner befitting His majesty, without resembling creation.

CHAPTER 3: BELIEF IN THE ANGELS
═══════════════════════════════

"All praise is due to Allah, Creator of the heavens and the earth, who made the angels messengers having wings." (Quran 35:1)

Angels are created from light. The Prophet ﷺ said: "The angels were created from light, the jinn from smokeless fire, and Adam from that which has been described to you." (Sahih Muslim 2996)

Key Angels:
1. Jibreel (Gabriel) عليه السلام — Angel of Revelation, brought the Quran to the Prophet ﷺ
   "Say: Whoever is an enemy to Jibreel — it is he who has brought it down upon your heart by permission of Allah." (Quran 2:97)
2. Mikail (Michael) عليه السلام — Angel of sustenance and rain
3. Israfil عليه السلام — Angel who will blow the Trumpet on the Day of Judgment
4. Malak al-Mawt (Angel of Death) — takes the souls at the time of death
5. Munkar and Nakir — angels who question the deceased in the grave
6. Raqib and Atid — recording angels (الكرام الكاتبين)
   "When the two receivers receive, seated on the right and on the left. Man does not utter any word except that with him is an observer prepared." (Quran 50:17–18)
7. Malik — keeper of Hellfire
8. Ridwan — keeper of Paradise

Functions:
- Recording deeds
- Guarding humans
- Delivering revelation
- Managing natural phenomena (rain, vegetation, death)
- Praising and worshipping Allah constantly

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 4: BELIEF IN THE DIVINE BOOKS
═══════════════════════════════

"He has sent down upon you the Book in truth, confirming what was before it. And He revealed the Torah and the Gospel before, as guidance for the people." (Quran 3:3–4)

Major Divine Books:
1. Suhuf (Scrolls) — given to Prophet Ibrahim عليه السلام
2. Tawrah (Torah) — given to Prophet Musa (Moses) عليه السلام
3. Zabur (Psalms) — given to Prophet Dawud (David) عليه السلام
4. Injil (Gospel) — given to Prophet Isa (Jesus) عليه السلام
5. Quran — given to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — the final, preserved, universal revelation
   "Indeed, it is We who sent down the reminder (Quran), and indeed, We will be its Guardian." (Quran 15:9)

The Quran's Unique Position:
- It is the only scripture that remains in its original form
- It abrogates all previous scriptures in matters of law
- It is a miracle in its language, prophecies, and scientific indications
- It will remain preserved until the Day of Judgment

CHAPTER 5: BELIEF IN THE PROPHETS AND MESSENGERS
═══════════════════════════════

"We have not sent any messenger except that he was to be obeyed by permission of Allah." (Quran 4:64)

Key beliefs:
- Prophets were chosen by Allah from among human beings
- They were protected from major sins (Ismah)
- They conveyed the message truthfully
- The first prophet was Adam عليه السلام, the last is Muhammad ﷺ
- 25 prophets are named in the Quran

Named Prophets in the Quran:
Adam, Idris (Enoch), Nuh (Noah), Hud, Salih, Ibrahim (Abraham), Lut (Lot), Ismail (Ishmael), Ishaq (Isaac), Yaqub (Jacob), Yusuf (Joseph), Ayyub (Job), Shu'ayb, Musa (Moses), Harun (Aaron), Dhul-Kifl, Dawud (David), Sulayman (Solomon), Ilyas (Elijah), Al-Yasa (Elisha), Yunus (Jonah), Zakariya (Zechariah), Yahya (John the Baptist), Isa (Jesus), Muhammad ﷺ

The Finality of Prophethood:
"Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the seal of the prophets." (Quran 33:40)
No new prophet will come after Muhammad ﷺ. This is a fundamental belief of Islam.

CHAPTER 6: BELIEF IN THE LAST DAY
═══════════════════════════════

"The Hour is coming — there is no doubt about it — and Allah will resurrect those in the graves." (Quran 22:7)

6.1 Events After Death
---
1. The soul is taken by the Angel of Death
2. Questioning in the grave by Munkar and Nakir: "Who is your Lord? What is your religion? Who was sent among you?"
3. The grave is either a garden of Paradise or a pit of Hellfire
4. The soul awaits in the Barzakh (realm between death and resurrection)

6.2 Major Signs of the Hour
---
The Prophet ﷺ mentioned the major signs (Sahih Muslim 2901):
1. Emergence of the Dajjal (the False Messiah)
2. Descent of Isa (Jesus) عليه السلام
3. Emergence of Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog)
4. The smoke (Dukhan)
5. The rising of the sun from the west
6. The beast (Dabbat al-Ard)
7. Three major landslides
8. A fire from Yemen driving people to the gathering place

6.3 The Day of Judgment
---
1. The Trumpet (As-Sur) will be blown
2. All creation will be resurrected
3. People will stand before Allah for judgment
4. The Scrolls of deeds will be distributed — right hand: Paradise; left hand: Hellfire
5. The Scale (Al-Mizan) — deeds will be weighed
6. The Bridge (As-Sirat) — over Hellfire, leading to Paradise
7. The Intercession (Ash-Shafa'ah) — the Prophet ﷺ will be given the greatest intercession
8. The Pool (Al-Hawd) — the Prophet's ﷺ Pool of Kawthar

CHAPTER 7: BELIEF IN DIVINE DECREE (QADR)
═══════════════════════════════

"Indeed, all things We created with predestination (Qadr)." (Quran 54:49)

Four Levels of Qadr:
1. Allah's Knowledge (Al-'Ilm): Allah knows everything that was, is, and will be.
2. Allah's Writing (Al-Kitabah): Everything is recorded in Al-Lawh Al-Mahfuz (The Preserved Tablet).
   "Didn't you know that Allah knows what is in the heaven and earth? Indeed, that is in a Record." (Quran 22:70)
3. Allah's Will (Al-Mashee'ah): Whatever Allah wills happens, and whatever He does not will does not happen.
4. Allah's Creation (Al-Khalq): Allah creates all things, including human actions.
   "And Allah created you and that which you do." (Quran 37:96)

Human Free Will:
- Humans have the ability to choose, but their choices are within Allah's decree
- We are held accountable for our choices
- We must strive and take action while trusting in Allah's plan (Tawakkul)
- The Prophet ﷺ said: "Tie your camel and trust in Allah." (Jami at-Tirmidhi 2517)

Responding to Qadr:
- Good things: Be grateful (Shukr)
- Difficult things: Be patient (Sabr) — "No fatigue, nor disease, nor sorrow, nor sadness, nor hurt, nor distress befalls a Muslim, even if it were the prick of a thorn, but Allah expiates some of his sins for that." (Sahih Bukhari 5641)
