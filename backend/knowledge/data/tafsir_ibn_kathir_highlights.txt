TAFSIR IBN KATHIR — KEY HIGHLIGHTS AND EXPLANATIONS
Source: Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Imam Ibn Kathir, d. 1373 CE), one of the most authentic and widely used Tafsir works

SURAH AL-FATIHA (1) — THE OPENING

Verse 1:1 — "In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful"
Tafsir: This Basmalah is the key to the Quran. Ar-Rahman indicates comprehensive mercy encompassing all creation, while Ar-Rahim refers to the special mercy reserved for believers. Beginning any action with Bismillah brings blessing.

Verse 1:2 — "All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the Worlds"
Tafsir: Al-Hamd encompasses praise, gratitude, and commendation. "Lord of the Worlds" means He is the Creator, Sustainer, and Master of everything — humans, jinn, angels, animals, and all creation.

Verse 1:5 — "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help"
Tafsir: This is the essence of Tawheed. Worship is exclusive to Allah, and seeking help is directed to Him alone. This verse separates between the rights of Allah (worship) and the needs of creation (help).

Verse 1:6-7 — "Guide us to the Straight Path, the path of those You have blessed"
Tafsir: Guidance (Hidayah) is the greatest blessing. The "Straight Path" is Islam. "Those You have blessed" refers to the Prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous (Quran 4:69). "Not those who earned anger" refers to those who knew the truth but rejected it. "Not those who went astray" refers to those who worship without knowledge.

SURAH AL-BAQARAH (2) — THE COW (Key Verses)

Verse 2:2-5 — Characteristics of the Righteous
Tafsir: Five qualities of the believers: belief in the unseen, establishing prayer, spending from what Allah has given, believing in all revelations, and certainty in the Hereafter. "Guidance" here means they are already guided AND will receive increasing guidance.

Verse 2:155-157 — Tests and Patience
"And We will surely test you with something of fear, hunger, loss of wealth, lives, and fruits; but give glad tidings to the patient."
Tafsir: Tests are inevitable. They come in five forms. Patience is rewarded with blessings, mercy, and being guided. "Those are the ones upon whom are blessings from their Lord and mercy. Those are the rightly guided." The patient receive three rewards: divine blessings, mercy, and guidance.

Verse 2:183 — Fasting Prescribed
"Fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, so that you may achieve Taqwa."
Tafsir: Fasting is not unique to Islam, confirming continuity with previous nations. The goal is Taqwa (God-consciousness), not mere hunger. Fasting trains the soul to resist desires and strengthens willpower.

Verse 2:255 — Ayat al-Kursi (The Verse of the Throne)
Tafsir: This is the greatest verse in the Quran (Sahih Muslim 810). It contains 10 complete sentences about Allah's attributes:
1. Allah — there is no deity except Him (Oneness)
2. The Ever-Living (He never dies)
3. The Sustainer of all existence
4. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him
5. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and earth
6. No one can intercede except by His permission
7. He knows what is before them and behind them
8. They encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills
9. His Throne extends over the heavens and earth
10. Their preservation does not tire Him — He is the Most High, the Greatest

Verse 2:286 — Last Verse of Al-Baqarah
"Allah does not burden a soul beyond that it can bear."
Tafsir: This is a mercy from Allah. Every test is within our capacity. The dua at the end — "Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget or err" — was answered by Allah with "I have done so" (Sahih Muslim 126).

SURAH ALI IMRAN (3) — FAMILY OF IMRAN

Verse 3:14 — The Love of Desires
"Beautified for people is the love of that which they desire — women, sons, heaped-up gold..."
Tafsir: These are tests, not inherently evil. The point is: do not let worldly desires distract from the Hereafter. What is with Allah is far better.

Verse 3:103 — Unity
"And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided."
Tafsir: The "rope of Allah" is the Quran and the Sunnah. Unity upon truth is a divine command. Division leads to weakness.

Verse 3:185 — Reality of Life
"Every soul will taste death, and you will only be given your compensation on the Day of Resurrection."
Tafsir: All worldly possessions are temporary. True success is being saved from the Fire and admitted to Paradise.

SURAH AN-NISA (4) — THE WOMEN

Verse 4:1 — Origin of Humanity
"O mankind, fear your Lord, who created you from one soul and created from it its mate."
Tafsir: All humanity shares a common origin. This establishes equality regardless of race, colour, or ethnicity.

Verse 4:36 — Rights and Kindness
"Worship Allah and associate nothing with Him, and be kind to parents, relatives, orphans, the needy, the near neighbour, the distant neighbour, the companion at your side, the traveler, and those your right hands possess."
Tafsir: This verse establishes a comprehensive system of social responsibility — from family to the wider community.

SURAH YASIN (36) — THE HEART OF THE QURAN

"The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Yasin.'" (Tirmidhi 2887 — though some scholars grade this weak, it is widely cited)

Verse 36:1-12 — Mission of the Messenger
Tafsir: The Quran confirms Muhammad ﷺ is on the Straight Path. His mission is to warn those who have not been warned before. Guidance is for those who fear Allah.

Verse 36:33-36 — Signs in Creation
"And a sign for them is the dead earth. We have brought it to life and brought forth grain."
Tafsir: The resurrection of dead land through rain is a proof of the resurrection of the dead on the Day of Judgment. He who brings dead land to life can bring dead bodies to life.

Verse 36:82 — The Power of "Kun Fayakun"
"His command is only when He intends a thing that He says to it, 'Be,' and it is."
Tafsir: Allah's power is absolute and instantaneous. He does not need causes or means. When He wills something, it simply is.

SURAH AR-RAHMAN (55) — THE MOST MERCIFUL

Verse 55:1-4 — "Ar-Rahman. Taught the Quran. Created man. Taught him eloquent speech."
Tafsir: The Surah opens with Allah's greatest name of mercy. The order is significant: mercy first, then the Quran, then creation. The Quran is a greater blessing than existence itself.

Verse 55:13 — "So which of the favours of your Lord would you deny?"
Tafsir: This refrain is repeated 31 times. It lists blessings systematically — from creation, to sustenance, to the sun and moon, to fruits, to Paradise — challenging creation to deny any single favour.

Theme: Every blessing demands recognition and gratitude. The contrast between the punishments of the deniers and the rewards of the righteous is central.

SURAH AL-MULK (67) — THE SOVEREIGNTY

Verse 67:1-2 — Purpose of Life and Death
"Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion... who created death and life to test you as to which of you is best in deed."
Tafsir: Life is a test. Allah does not say "most deeds" but "best in deed" — quality over quantity. Death was mentioned before life because it is the greater motivator.

Verse 67:3-4 — Perfection of Creation
"You do not see in the creation of the Most Merciful any inconsistency. So return your vision — do you see any breaks?"
Tafsir: The universe is flawless in design. This challenges people to look at the sky and creation and find even one defect — they will not.

SURAH AL-IKHLAS (112) — PURE MONOTHEISM

"Say: He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born. And there is none equivalent to Him."
Tafsir: This Surah is equal to one-third of the Quran (Sahih Bukhari 5015) because the Quran's themes are three: rulings, stories, and theology — this Surah embodies pure theology.

Key Points:
- "The One" — absolute oneness, no partner, no division, no plurality
- "The Eternal Refuge" — everything needs Him, He needs nothing
- "He neither begets nor is born" — refutes claims of divine offspring
- "None equivalent" — no comparison, no likeness, no equal
