ISLAMIC ETHICS & AKHLAQ: THE PROPHETIC CHARACTER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Source: Shamail al-Muhammadiyah, Adab al-Mufrad, Ihya Ulum al-Din
Purpose: Focus on internal character (Akhlaq) and social etiquette (Adab).

Section 1: The Essence of Character
---
The Prophet ﷺ said: "I was sent only to perfect noble character." (Malik's Muwatta)
The heaviest thing on the Scale of the believer on the Day of Resurrection will be good character.

1.1 Truthfulness (Sidq)
- The fundamental trait of a believer. 
- "Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise." (Sahih Bukhari)
- Being truthful in words, intentions, and deeds.

1.2 Trustworthiness (Amanah)
- A believer is one whom people trust with their wealth and lives.
- Fulfilling promises and keeping secrets.
- Avoiding treachery even towards those who have betrayed you.

Section 2: Social Manners (Adab)
---
2.1 Controlling the Tongue
- "Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent." (Sahih Muslim)
- Avoiding backbiting (Gheebah), slander (Naminah), and foul language.
- The definition of backbiting: "Mentioning your brother with something he dislikes."

2.2 Humility vs. Arrogance (Kibr)
- "No one with an atom's weight of arrogance in his heart will enter Paradise."
- Arrogance is defined as "rejecting the truth and looking down upon people."
- The Prophet ﷺ used to sit with the poor and eat with them, showing ultimate humility.

2.3 Patience and Forgiveness (Sabr & Afw)
- Forgiving those who wrong you is the peak of character.
- The Prophet ﷺ forgave the people of Ta'if despite their physical abuse.
- Courage is not in physical strength, but in "controlling oneself during a fit of rage."

Section 3: Family and Community
---
3.1 Kindness to Families
- "The best of you are those who are best to their families."
- Respecting the elderly and being merciful to the young.

3.2 Maintaining Ties (Silat al-Rahim)
- Cutting off family ties is a major sin.
- Connecting with those who cut you off is the true "Silat al-Rahim."

3.3 Helping Others
- "Allah is in the aid of His servant as long as the servant is in the aid of his brother."
- Removing a harmful object from the road is a form of Sadaqah (Charity).
