SECTION 1: AQEEDAH (THE SIX PILLARS OF IMAN)
═══════════════════════════════

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Faith is to believe in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and to believe in divine decree, both its good and its evil." (Sahih Muslim 8)

1.1 Belief in Allah (Tawheed)
---
- Definition: Firm belief that Allah is the One and Only God, the Creator, the Sustainer, and the Only One worthy of worship.
- Three Categories of Tawheed:
  1. Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah: Oneness of Allah in His Lordship (as Creator and Provider).
  2. Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah: Oneness of Allah in Worship (directing all acts of worship to Him alone).
  3. Tawheed al-Asma was-Sifat: Oneness of Allah in His Names and Attributes.

1.2 Belief in the Angels (Mala'ikah)
---
- Created from light. They do not have free will and obey Allah perfectly.
- Key Angels:
  - Jibreel (Gabriel): Responsible for bringing revelation to the Prophets.
  - Mika'il (Michael): Responsible for rain and sustenance.
  - Israfil: Responsible for blowing the Trumpet on the Day of Judgment.
  - Malak al-Mawt: The Angel of Death.

1.3 Belief in the Revealed Books (Kutub)
---
- Allah sent scriptures to various Messengers for guidance:
  - The Suhuf (Scrolls) to Ibrahim (Abraham).
  - The Tawrat (Torah) to Musa (Moses).
  - The Zabur (Psalms) to Dawud (David).
  - The Injeel (Gospel) to Isa (Jesus).
  - The Quran to Muhammad ﷺ (The final, protected, and complete revelation).

1.4 Belief in the Messengers (Rusul)
---
- Allah sent approximately 124,000 Prophets, of whom 25 are mentioned by name in the Quran.
- The Five Great Prophets (Ulul Azm): Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, and Muhammad ﷺ.
- Muhammad ﷺ is the final Prophet (Khatam-an-Nabiyyin).

1.5 Belief in the Day of Judgment (Yawm al-Qiyamah)
---
- Belief that everyone will be resurrected and held accountable for their deeds.
- Key concepts: The Scale (Mizan), the Bridge (Sirat), Intercession (Shafa'ah), Paradise (Jannah), and Hell (Jahannam).

1.6 Belief in Divine Decree (Al-Qadr)
---
- Belief that Allah knows everything that will happen, has written it down, wills it to happen, and creates it. 
- Paradox of Will: Mankind has free choice and responsibility, but it all happens within the knowledge and power of Allah.

SECTION 2: IBADA (THE FIVE PILLARS OF ISLAM)
═══════════════════════════════

2.1 Shahadah (Testimony of Faith)
---
"Ash-hadu alla ilaha illallah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah."
(I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah).

2.2 Salah (The Five Daily Prayers)
---
- The second pillar and the first thing questioned on the Day of Judgment.
- The Five Prayers:
  1. Fajr: Before sunrise.
  2. Dhuhr: After midday.
  3. Asr: Afternoon.
  4. Maghrib: After sunset.
  5. Isha: Nightfall.
- Conditions: Purity (Wudu), facing the Qibla (Makkah), covering the Awrah, and the correct time.

2.3 Zakat (Obligatory Charity)
---
- Paying 2.5% of annual savings above the Nisab (minimum threshold) to eight categories of needy people.
- It purifies wealth and levels social inequality.

2.4 Sawm (Fasting in Ramadan)
---
- Abstaining from food, drink, and intimacy from dawn to sunset during the month of Ramadan.
- Purpose: To develop Taqwa (God-consciousness) and empathy for the poor.

2.5 Hajj (Pilgrimage to Makkah)
---
- Obligatory once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able.

SECTION 3: TAHARAH (PURIFICATION - THE WUDU METHOD)
═══════════════════════════════

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Purity is half of faith." (Sahih Muslim 223)

Step-by-Step Wudu (Ablution):
1. Niyyah (Intention) in the heart.
2. Say "Bismillah" (In the name of Allah).
3. Wash hands up to the wrists 3 times.
4. Rinse the mouth 3 times (Madmadah).
5. Sniff water into the nose and blow out 3 times (Istinshaq).
6. Wash the face from forehead to chin and ear to ear 3 times.
7. Wash arms up to and including the elbows 3 times (right first).
8. Wipe the head (Mash) once — from front to back and back to front.
9. Wipe the inside and outside of the ears once.
10. Wash feet up to and including the ankles 3 times (right first).

SECTION 4: AKHLAQ (CHARACTER & SOCIAL ETIQUETTE)
═══════════════════════════════

4.1 Rights of Parents
---
"Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be kind to parents." (Quran 17:23)
- Disobedience (Uquq) to parents is one of the major sins.

4.2 Rights of Neighbors
---
The Prophet ﷺ said: "He is not a believer whose neighbor is not safe from his harm." (Sahih Bukhari 6016)
- This applies regardless of the neighbor's faith.

4.3 Social Justice and Equality
---
"O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female and made you peoples and tribes that you may know one another. Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you." (Quran 49:13)
