ISLAMIC ETHICS AND AKHLAQ (CHARACTER) — COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE
Source: Quran, Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, classical texts on Islamic morality

CHAPTER 1: THE IMPORTANCE OF CHARACTER IN ISLAM

"And indeed, you [Muhammad] are of a great moral character." (Quran 68:4)
"The most beloved of you to me and the closest to me on the Day of Resurrection are those with the best character." (Tirmidhi 2018)
"Nothing is heavier on the Scale than good character." (Abu Dawud 4799)
"I was sent to perfect good character." (Musnad Ahmad, Al-Adab Al-Mufrad)

CHAPTER 2: PATIENCE (SABR) — الصبر

"O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer." (Quran 2:153)
"Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account." (Quran 39:10)
"No fatigue, nor disease, nor sorrow, nor sadness, nor hurt, nor distress befalls a Muslim, even if it were the prick of a thorn, but Allah expiates some of his sins for that." (Sahih Bukhari 5641)

Types of Sabr:
1. Patience in obedience to Allah (performing worship consistently)
2. Patience in avoiding sins (resisting temptation)
3. Patience with the decrees of Allah (accepting trials)

CHAPTER 3: GRATITUDE (SHUKR) — الشكر

"If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." (Quran 14:7)
"He who does not thank people has not thanked Allah." (Abu Dawud 4811)
"How wonderful is the affair of the believer, for all his affairs are good. If something good happens to him, he is grateful and that is good for him. If something bad happens to him, he is patient and that is good for him." (Sahih Muslim 2999)

Ways to show Shukr: with the heart (acknowledging blessings), with the tongue (praising Allah), with actions (using blessings in obedience).

CHAPTER 4: TRUTHFULNESS (SIDQ) — الصدق

"O you who have believed, fear Allah and be with those who are truthful." (Quran 9:119)
"Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise. A man keeps telling the truth until he is recorded with Allah as a Siddiq (truthful person). Lying leads to wickedness and wickedness leads to the Fire." (Sahih Bukhari 6094)

CHAPTER 5: KINDNESS TO PARENTS (BIRR AL-WALIDAYN) — بر الوالدين

"And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment." (Quran 17:23)
"And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say, 'My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.'" (Quran 17:24)

A man asked the Prophet ﷺ: "Who deserves my best companionship?" He said: "Your mother." The man asked again. He said: "Your mother." The man asked again. He said: "Your mother." The man asked again. He said: "Then your father." (Sahih Bukhari 5971)

Even after death: making dua for them, giving charity on their behalf, maintaining ties with their friends.

CHAPTER 6: RIGHTS OF NEIGHBOURS

"Jibreel kept recommending me to treat my neighbour well until I thought he would tell me to make him an heir." (Sahih Bukhari 6014)
"He is not a believer whose stomach is filled while his neighbour goes hungry." (Al-Adab Al-Mufrad 112)
"By Allah, he does not believe! By Allah, he does not believe! He whose neighbour does not feel safe from his mischief." (Sahih Bukhari 6016)

Neighbours' rights: greeting them, visiting the sick, attending funerals, sharing food, not harming them, not peeking into their homes, not building higher to block their air without permission.

CHAPTER 7: BACKBITING (GHEEBAH) AND SLANDER

"O you who have believed, avoid much assumption. Indeed, some assumption is sin. And do not spy or backbite each other. Would one of you like to eat the flesh of his brother when dead?" (Quran 49:12)

Definition: The Prophet ﷺ said: "Do you know what backbiting is?" They said: "Allah and His Messenger know best." He said: "Mentioning your brother with something he dislikes." They said: "What if what I say is true?" He said: "If it is true, you have backbitten him. If it is not true, you have slandered him." (Sahih Muslim 2589)

CHAPTER 8: HUMILITY AND ARROGANCE

"And do not walk upon the earth exultantly. Indeed, you will never tear the earth apart, and you will never reach the mountains in height." (Quran 17:37)
"No one who has the weight of a seed of arrogance in his heart will enter Paradise." (Sahih Muslim 91)
"Allah has revealed to me that you must be humble so that no one boasts over another." (Sahih Muslim 2865)

Signs of arrogance: rejecting the truth, looking down on people, thinking oneself better due to wealth/status/lineage.

CHAPTER 9: GENEROSITY (KARAM) — الكرم

"The upper hand (the giving hand) is better than the lower hand (the receiving hand)." (Sahih Bukhari 1427)
"The generous is close to Allah, close to people, close to Paradise, far from the Fire." (Tirmidhi 1961)
The Prophet ﷺ was "more generous than the wind that brings rain" (Sahih Bukhari 6).

CHAPTER 10: MODESTY (HAYA) — الحياء

"Haya (modesty) is a branch of faith." (Sahih Bukhari 9)
"Haya does not bring anything except good." (Sahih Bukhari 6117)
"If you feel no shame, then do as you wish." (Sahih Bukhari 3483)
Modesty includes: modesty before Allah (being ashamed to sin), modesty in dress, modesty in speech and behaviour.

CHAPTER 11: JUSTICE AND FAIRNESS (ADL) — العدل

"O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves, your parents, or relatives." (Quran 4:135)
"Seven will be shaded by Allah on the Day when there is no shade except His shade... a just leader." (Sahih Bukhari 660)

CHAPTER 12: FORGIVENESS AND PARDONING

"And let them pardon and overlook. Would you not like that Allah should forgive you?" (Quran 24:22)
"The strong man is not one who wrestles well but the one who controls himself when he is in a fit of rage." (Sahih Bukhari 6114)
"Whoever suppresses his anger when he has the ability to enforce it, Allah will call him before all of creation on the Day of Resurrection." (Tirmidhi 2021)

CHAPTER 13: BUSINESS ETHICS IN ISLAM

"O you who have believed, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly but only in lawful business by mutual consent." (Quran 4:29)
"The truthful, honest merchant is with the prophets, the truthful, and the martyrs." (Tirmidhi 1209)

Prohibited: cheating in trade, hiding defects, hoarding, price manipulation, false advertising, bribery.
Required: honest measurement, clear contracts, honouring agreements, fair wages, paying the worker before his sweat dries (Ibn Majah 2443).

CHAPTER 14: ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP

"And do not cause corruption on earth after it has been set in order." (Quran 7:56)
"If the Hour is about to come and one of you has a seedling in his hand, let him plant it." (Musnad Ahmad)
"Whoever plants a tree, no human or creature eats from it except that it is charity for him." (Sahih Muslim 1553)

CHAPTER 15: SOCIAL CONDUCT

Greeting: "Spread the greeting of peace amongst yourselves." (Sahih Muslim 54)
Smiling: "Your smiling in the face of your brother is charity." (Tirmidhi 1956)
Removing harm from the path: "Faith has 73+ branches; the highest is La ilaha illallah, the lowest is removing something harmful from the road." (Sahih Muslim 35)
Visiting the sick, attending funerals, responding to invitations, saying Yarhamukallah to one who sneezes.
