SEERAH — BIOGRAPHY OF PROPHET MUHAMMAD ﷺ
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Source: Quran, Sahih Hadith, classical Seerah works (Ibn Ishaq, Ibn Hisham, Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum)
Coverage: Life of the Prophet ﷺ from birth to passing, character, key events, companions

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 1: EARLY LIFE AND PROPHETHOOD
═══════════════════════════════

1.1 Birth and Lineage
---
Full name: Muhammad ibn Abdullah ibn Abdul-Muttalib ibn Hashim
Born: 12th Rabi' al-Awwal, Year of the Elephant (approximately 570 CE), Makkah
Tribe: Quraysh — the noblest tribe of Arabia
Father: Abdullah ibn Abdul-Muttalib (died before the Prophet's birth)
Mother: Aminah bint Wahb (died when he was 6 years old)
Foster mother: Halimah as-Sa'diyyah (nursed him in the desert as was custom)
Guardian: Abdul-Muttalib (grandfather, until age 8), then Abu Talib (uncle)

1.2 Youth and Character Before Prophethood
---
Known as "Al-Amin" (The Trustworthy) and "As-Sadiq" (The Truthful) even before revelation.
He never worshipped idols, never drank alcohol, and was known for his honesty in trade.
At age 25, he married Khadijah bint Khuwaylid رضي الله عنها, a successful businesswoman 15 years his senior. She was the first to believe in him.

1.3 The First Revelation (610 CE)
---
At age 40, while meditating in the Cave of Hira on Mount Nur, Angel Jibreel appeared:
"Read! In the name of your Lord who created. Created man from a clinging substance. Read! And your Lord is the Most Generous. Who taught by the pen. Taught man that which he knew not." (Quran 96:1–5)

This was the beginning of 23 years of revelation. The Prophet ﷺ was trembling and went to Khadijah, who comforted him saying: "By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you. You keep good relations with your relatives, you bear the burden of the weak, you help the poor and the needy, you entertain guests, and you help against the calamities that affect people." (Sahih Bukhari 3)

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 2: THE MAKKAN PERIOD (610–622 CE)
═══════════════════════════════

2.1 Secret Da'wah (First 3 Years)
---
Early believers: Khadijah (wife), Ali ibn Abi Talib (cousin, age 10), Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (best friend), Zayd ibn Harithah (freed slave/adopted son), Bilal ibn Rabah.
They prayed in secret at Dar al-Arqam (the house of Al-Arqam).

2.2 Public Da'wah and Persecution
---
When the Prophet ﷺ publicly called to Islam, the Quraysh responded with:
- Mockery and insults
- Physical torture of weak Muslims (Bilal was placed under hot rocks; Sumayyah bint Khayyat was martyred — the first martyr in Islam)
- Social and economic boycott of Banu Hashim (lasted 3 years)
- Assassination attempt (which led to the Hijrah)

The Prophet's ﷺ patience: He never retaliated with violence during the Makkan period. When the people of Ta'if pelted him with stones, the Angel of the Mountains offered to crush them between the two mountains. The Prophet ﷺ refused, saying: "Perhaps Allah will bring from their progeny those who worship Him." (Sahih Bukhari 3231)

2.3 The Year of Grief (619 CE)
---
Both Khadijah رضي الله عنها and Abu Talib died in the same year.
Khadijah was the Prophet's ﷺ greatest supporter for 25 years.
Abu Talib had provided tribal protection even though he did not embrace Islam.

2.4 Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj (The Night Journey and Ascension)
---
"Exalted is He who took His Servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa." (Quran 17:1)
- The Prophet ﷺ was taken from Makkah to Jerusalem (Al-Aqsa) on the Buraq
- He led all the prophets in prayer at Al-Aqsa
- He ascended through the seven heavens meeting prophets: Adam, Isa & Yahya, Yusuf, Idris, Harun, Musa, Ibrahim
- The five daily prayers were prescribed during this journey (originally 50, reduced to 5 with the reward of 50)
- This event occurred approximately one year before the Hijrah

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 3: THE HIJRAH AND MADINAN PERIOD (622–632 CE)
═══════════════════════════════

3.1 The Hijrah (Migration to Madinah, 622 CE)
---
After receiving permission from Allah, the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr migrated to Madinah, hiding in the Cave of Thawr (Ghar Thawr) for three days.
"If you do not help him, Allah has already aided him when those who disbelieved drove him out... when they were in the cave and he said to his companion, 'Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us.'" (Quran 9:40)

The Hijrah marks the beginning of the Islamic calendar.

3.2 Establishing the Islamic State
---
Upon arrival in Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ:
1. Built Masjid an-Nabawi (the Prophet's Mosque)
2. Established the brotherhood between Muhajirun (Makkan emigrants) and Ansar (Madinan helpers)
3. Drafted the Constitution of Madinah — a treaty outlining rights and obligations for all citizens, including Muslims, Jews, and pagans

3.3 Major Battles
---

Battle of Badr (624 CE / 2 AH):
- First major battle of Islam
- 313 Muslims vs. 1,000 Quraysh
- Decisive Muslim victory — Allah sent angels to assist
- "Already there has been for you a sign in the two armies which met — one fighting in the cause of Allah and another of disbelievers." (Quran 3:13)
- 70 Quraysh leaders killed, 70 captured

Battle of Uhud (625 CE / 3 AH):
- 700 Muslims vs. 3,000 Quraysh
- Initial Muslim advantage turned when archers left their posts
- The Prophet ﷺ was injured (face wounded, tooth broken)
- 70 Muslims martyred including Hamza ibn Abdul-Muttalib (the Prophet's uncle)
- Lesson: Obedience to the Prophet ﷺ and discipline are essential

Battle of the Trench (Khandaq, 627 CE / 5 AH):
- 3,000 Muslims vs. 10,000 Confederate forces
- Salman al-Farisi suggested digging a trench (a Persian tactic new to Arabs)
- After a prolonged siege, a wind and angel intervention scattered the Confederates
- "O you who have believed, remember the favor of Allah upon you when armies came to you and We sent upon them a wind and armies you did not see." (Quran 33:9)

3.4 The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (628 CE / 6 AH)
---
- A peace treaty between Muslims and Quraysh
- Initially appeared unfavorable to Muslims
- Allah called it a "Clear Victory" (Quran 48:1)
- It led to peace, which allowed Islam to spread rapidly — more people embraced Islam in the 2 years after this treaty than in all previous years combined

3.5 The Conquest of Makkah (630 CE / 8 AH)
---
- After Quraysh violated the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, the Prophet ﷺ marched with 10,000 Muslims
- Makkah was conquered virtually without bloodshed
- The Prophet ﷺ entered humbly, head bowed, and declared general amnesty
- He entered the Ka'bah and destroyed 360 idols, reciting: "Truth has come and falsehood has departed. Indeed, falsehood is ever bound to depart." (Quran 17:81)
- He forgave even his worst enemies, including Hind who had mutilated Hamza's body

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 4: THE FAREWELL SERMON AND PASSING
═══════════════════════════════

4.1 The Farewell Pilgrimage (632 CE / 10 AH)
---
The Prophet ﷺ performed Hajj with over 100,000 companions and delivered the Farewell Sermon at Mount Arafat:

Key points from the Farewell Khutbah:
1. "All mankind is from Adam and Eve. An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab over an Arab. A white person has no superiority over a black person, nor a black person over a white — except by piety and good action."
2. "O People, your lives, your property, and your honour are sacred — as sacred as this day, this month, this city."
3. "Treat your women well and be kind to them."
4. "I leave behind me two things — the Quran and my Sunnah — you will never go astray if you follow them."
5. "All those who listen to me shall pass on my words to others, and those to others again. The last ones may understand my words better than those who listen directly."

Then the verse was revealed: "This day I have perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have approved for you Islam as religion." (Quran 5:3)

4.2 The Passing of the Prophet ﷺ (632 CE / 12th Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH)
---
The Prophet ﷺ fell ill with a fever. He continued to pray and lead prayers as long as he could.
His final words included: "The prayer, the prayer! And fear Allah regarding those whom your right hands possess." (Abu Dawud 5156)
He passed away with his head in Aisha's رضي الله عنها lap, saying: "Rather, the Highest Companion" (Sahih Bukhari 4437)

Abu Bakr addressed the people: "Whoever worshipped Muhammad, then Muhammad has died. And whoever worshipped Allah, then Allah is alive and will never die." He then recited: "Muhammad is not but a messenger. Other messengers have passed on before him. So if he was to die or be killed, would you turn back on your heels?" (Quran 3:144)

CHAPTER 5: CHARACTER AND QUALITIES OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
═══════════════════════════════

"And indeed, you are of a great moral character." (Quran 68:4)
"You have in the Messenger of Allah a beautiful example." (Quran 33:21)

Physical Appearance:
- Medium height, neither very tall nor short
- Broad-shouldered with a wide chest
- Luminous face, described as more beautiful than the full moon (Sahih Bukhari 3549)
- Large dark eyes with long eyelashes
- Seal of Prophethood between his shoulder blades

Character Traits:
- Truthful — never lied in his entire life
- Generous — "He was more generous than the wind that brings rain" (Sahih Bukhari 6)
- Brave — always at the front of battles
- Humble — mended his own shoes, milked his goat, served his family (Sahih Bukhari 676)
- Merciful — "I was sent as a mercy to all of creation" (Quran 21:107)
- Patient — endured years of persecution without retaliation
- Forgiving — forgave those who harmed him, including the people of Ta'if and the Quraysh at the Conquest of Makkah
- Just — "Even if Fatimah the daughter of Muhammad stole, I would cut her hand" (Sahih Bukhari 3475)
- Compassionate to children — kissed his grandchildren, played with them, shortened prayers when hearing a baby cry (Sahih Bukhari 707)
- Kind to animals — "A woman was tortured in Hell because she tied a cat until it died. She neither fed it nor let it eat from the vermin of the earth." (Sahih Bukhari 3318)

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 6: THE COMPANIONS (SAHABAH)
═══════════════════════════════

"You are the best nation produced for mankind." (Quran 3:110)
The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best of people is my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them." (Sahih Bukhari 2652)

The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs (Al-Khulafa ar-Rashidun):
1. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq رضي الله عنه (632–634 CE) — The Truthful. First adult male to accept Islam. Closest companion. United Arabia after the Prophet's ﷺ death.
2. Umar ibn al-Khattab رضي الله عنه (634–644 CE) — The Just. Expanded the Islamic state to Persia, Egypt, and Syria. Established the Islamic calendar, treasury, and judicial system.
3. Uthman ibn Affan رضي الله عنه (644–656 CE) — Compiled the standard Quran (Mushaf). Known for generosity.
4. Ali ibn Abi Talib رضي الله عنه (656–661 CE) — The Gate of Knowledge. Cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ.

Notable Female Companions:
- Khadijah bint Khuwaylid رضي الله عنها — first believer, the Prophet's ﷺ greatest supporter
- Aisha bint Abu Bakr رضي الله عنها — scholar of Islam, narrated over 2,200 hadiths
- Fatimah az-Zahra رضي الله عنها — daughter of the Prophet ﷺ, leader of the women of Paradise
- Sumayyah bint Khayyat رضي الله عنها — first martyr of Islam
- Umm Salamah رضي الله عنها — advisor to the Prophet ﷺ, her counsel saved the day at Hudaybiyyah

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 7: MIRACLES (MU'JIZAT) OF THE PROPHET ﷺ
═══════════════════════════════

The Prophet ﷺ was granted numerous miracles by Allah as proofs of his Prophethood. The greatest and eternal miracle is the Quran.

7.1 The Noble Quran
---
The Quran is a linguistic, scientific, and spiritual miracle that remains unchanged. "We have sent down the Reminder, and We will surely preserve it." (Quran 15:9)

7.2 Splitting of the Moon (Inshaqaq al-Qamar)
---
When the Quraysh demanded a sign, the Prophet ﷺ pointed to the moon and it split into two halves. "The Hour has come near, and the moon has split." (Quran 54:1)

7.3 Water Flowing from Fingers
---
On several occasions (like the Day of Hudaybiyyah), during a shortage of water, the Prophet ﷺ placed his hands in a vessel and water gushed forth from his fingers, allowing hundreds to drink and perform Wudu. (Sahih Bukhari 3572)

7.4 Increasing Food
---
In the Battle of the Trench, Jabir (RA) prepared a small amount of food for the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ called the entire army (1000 men), and everyone ate till they were full from a single pot that remained full. (Sahih Bukhari 4101)

7.5 Healing the Sick
---
The Prophet ﷺ healed the eye of Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA) at Khaybar and the broken leg of Abdullah ibn Atik by his touch and Dua.

═══════════════════════════════
CHAPTER 8: SHAMAIL — THE PROPHETIC CHARACTER & CONDUCT
═══════════════════════════════

8.1 His Humility
---
He was the leader of humanity but would sit on the ground, eat with the poor, and never allowed people to stand up for him out of reverence. "I am only a servant; I eat as a servant eats and I sit as a servant sits." (As-Shamail al-Muhammadiyah)

8.2 His Bravery
---
Ali (RA) said: "When the battle became intense, we would seek shelter behind the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, for he was the closest to the enemy." (Musnad Ahmad)

8.3 His Mercy to Animals
---
He forbade the branding of animals on the face and once comforted a camel that was crying from overwork and hunger, reprimanding its owner. (Abu Dawud 2549)

8.4 99 Names of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Select)
---
1. Muhammad (The Praised One)
2. Ahmad (Most Deserving of Praise)
3. Al-Aqib (The Final One)
4. Al-Hashir (The Gatherer)
5. Al-Mahi (The Eraser of Disbelief)
6. As-Sadiq (The Truthful)
7. Al-Amin (The Trustworthy)
8. Rahmatal-lil-Alamin (Mercy to the Worlds)
