THE AFTEERLIFE: JANNAH AND JAHANNAM IN ISLAMIC TRADITION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Source: Quran (Surah Ar-Rahman, Al-Waqi'ah), Sahih Bukhari (Book of Heart-softeners)
Purpose: Scholarly reference for eschatological depth.

Section 1: The Description of Paradise (Jannah)
---
1.1 The Ultimate Reward
- Allah says: "I have prepared for My righteous slaves what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no mind has conceived." (Hadith Qudsi)
- In Jannah, there is no sickness, no death, no aging, and no sorrow.

1.2 The Gates and Levels
- There are 8 gates of Jannah. One is called 'Ar-Rayyan', reserved for those who fast.
- There are 100 levels between each level and the next like the distance between heaven and earth. The highest is Firdaws.

1.3 Life in Jannah
- Rivers of milk, honey, and pure water.
- Palaces made of gold and silver bricks.
- No "useless talk" — only "Peace, peace!" (Salam, Salam).
- The greatest reward: Beholding the Countenance of Allah (Looking at Allah).

Section 2: The Description of Hellfire (Jahannam)
---
2.1 The Severity of Punishment
- The fire of this world is only one-seventieth (1/70) part of the heat of Hellfire.
- Fuel of Hellfire is "men and stones." (Quran 2:24)

2.2 The Depths and Inhabitants
- Jahannam is deep; a stone thrown into it would take 70 years to reach the bottom.
- Food of the inhabitants: Az-Zaqqum (a bitter, thorny tree) and Ghisleen.
- Drink: Hamim (boiling water) that cuts through internal organs.

2.3 The Mercy and Refuge
- The "People of A'raf" — those whose good and bad deeds are equal.
- Seeking refuge (Dua): "Our Lord, avert from us the punishment of Hell. Indeed, its punishment is ever adhering." (Quran 25:65)

Section 3: The Intermediate State (Barzakh)
---
3.1 Life in the Grave
- Everyone will be questioned by Munkar and Nakir.
- The grave is either "a meadow from the meadows of Paradise or a pit from the pits of Hell."

3.2 Preparation for the Last Day
- The Prophet ﷺ said: "Wise is the one who takes account of himself and works for what comes after death."
