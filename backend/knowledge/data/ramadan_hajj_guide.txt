RAMADAN AND HAJJ — COMPLETE WORSHIP GUIDE
Source: Quran, Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, classical Fiqh references

CHAPTER 1: RAMADAN — THE MONTH OF MERCY

"The month of Ramadan in which was revealed the Quran, a guidance for the people and clear proofs of guidance and criterion." (Quran 2:185)

Virtues of Ramadan:
- Gates of Paradise are opened, gates of Hell are closed, devils are chained (Sahih Bukhari 1899)
- "Whoever fasts Ramadan out of faith and seeking reward, his past sins are forgiven." (Sahih Bukhari 38)
- "Whoever stands in prayer in Ramadan out of faith and seeking reward, his past sins are forgiven." (Sahih Bukhari 37)
- Contains Laylatul Qadr (Night of Decree) — better than 1,000 months (Quran 97:3)

1.1 Suhoor (Pre-Dawn Meal)
"Take Suhoor, for in Suhoor there is blessing." (Sahih Bukhari 1923)
Best time: as close to Fajr as possible. Dates and water are recommended.

1.2 Iftar (Breaking the Fast)
"The people will remain upon goodness as long as they hasten to break the fast." (Sahih Bukhari 1957)
Sunnah: Break with dates and water. Dua: "Dhahaba az-zama'u wabtallatil-uruqu wa thabatal-ajru in sha'Allah" (Abu Dawud 2357)

1.3 Taraweeh Prayer
Sunnah Mu'akkadah (strongly recommended) — performed after Isha prayer during Ramadan.
Number of Raka'at: 8 (based on Aisha's hadith) or 20 (the practice of Umar and the majority). Both are valid.
"Whoever prays Qiyam (night prayer) in Ramadan with faith and seeking reward, his past sins are forgiven." (Sahih Bukhari 37)

1.4 I'tikaf (Spiritual Retreat)
Seclusion in the mosque during the last 10 days of Ramadan.
"The Prophet ﷺ used to observe I'tikaf in the last ten days of Ramadan." (Sahih Bukhari 2025)
Purpose: seek Laylatul Qadr, increase worship, disconnect from worldly distractions.

1.5 Laylatul Qadr (Night of Decree)
"Indeed, We sent the Quran down during the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months." (Quran 97:1-3)
When: Odd nights of the last 10 days (21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th, 29th). Most scholars emphasize the 27th night.
Best dua: "Allahumma innaka Afuwwun tuhibbul-afwa fa'fu anni" (Tirmidhi 3513)

1.6 Zakat al-Fitr
Obligatory before Eid prayer. One Sa' (approx 2.5-3 kg) of staple food per person.
"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ enjoined Zakatul-Fitr as purification for the fasting person." (Abu Dawud 1609)

1.7 Eid al-Fitr
Sunnahs of Eid:
- Ghusl (bathing)
- Wearing best clothes
- Eating dates before going to prayer (odd number)
- Going to prayer by one route and returning by another
- Takbir: "Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallahu, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, wa lillahil-hamd"

CHAPTER 2: HAJJ — THE FIFTH PILLAR

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97)
"Whoever performs Hajj and does not commit obscenity or transgression will return as on the day his mother bore him." (Sahih Bukhari 1521)

2.1 Types of Hajj
- Ifrad: Hajj only
- Tamattu': Umrah first, break Ihram, then Hajj (most recommended for overseas pilgrims). Sacrifice required.
- Qiran: Hajj and Umrah with one Ihram. Sacrifice required.

2.2 Miqat (Designated Points for Ihram)
- Dhul-Hulayfah (Abyar Ali): for people of Madinah
- Al-Juhfah (Rabigh): for people of Syria, Egypt, North Africa
- Qarn al-Manazil: for people of Najd
- Yalamlam: for people of Yemen
- Dhat Irq: for people of Iraq

2.3 Step-by-Step Hajj Guide (Tamattu' — most common)

Day 1: 8th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm at-Tarwiyah)
- Enter Ihram from Makkah
- Say "Labbayka Allahumma Hajjan" (Here I am, O Allah, for Hajj)
- Go to Mina
- Pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and Fajr (shortened but not combined)
- Spend the night at Mina

Day 2: 9th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm Arafah — THE MOST IMPORTANT DAY)
- After Fajr, proceed to Arafat
- Stand at Arafat from after Dhuhr until sunset
- "Hajj is Arafat." (Abu Dawud 1949)
- Combine and shorten Dhuhr and Asr (2 Rakat each, one Adhan, two Iqamas)
- Engage in intense Dua, Dhikr, and Istighfar — "The best dua is the dua on the Day of Arafah" (Tirmidhi 3585)
- After sunset, move to Muzdalifah
- At Muzdalifah: Pray Maghrib and Isha combined (shortened), collect pebbles (49 or 70), spend the night

Day 3: 10th Dhul-Hijjah (Yawm an-Nahr — Eid al-Adha)
- After Fajr, go to Mina
- Stone Jamrat al-Aqabah (the large pillar) — 7 pebbles, saying "Allahu Akbar" each
- Sacrifice an animal (Hady)
- Shave or trim hair (men shave; women cut a fingertip length)
- Partial release from Ihram (everything except spousal relations allowed)
- Perform Tawaf al-Ifadah (circumambulation) + Sa'i between Safa and Marwa
- Full release from Ihram

Days 4-5: 11th-12th Dhul-Hijjah (Ayyam at-Tashreeq)
- Stone all three Jamarat each day (7 pebbles each, starting with the smallest)
- Stay in Mina overnight
- On 12th: may leave Mina before sunset (for those in a hurry) or stay for the 13th (preferred)

Final: Tawaf al-Wada (Farewell Tawaf)
- Last act before leaving Makkah
- "No one should depart until the last thing they do is Tawaf of the House." (Sahih Muslim 1327)

CHAPTER 3: UMRAH — THE MINOR PILGRIMAGE

Steps of Umrah:
1. Enter Ihram at the Miqat with intention
2. Say "Labbayka Allahumma Umratan"
3. Perform Tawaf (7 circuits around the Kaaba)
4. Pray 2 Rakat behind Maqam Ibrahim
5. Perform Sa'i (7 trips between Safa and Marwa)
6. Shave or cut hair
7. Exit Ihram

"Umrah to Umrah is expiation for what comes between them." (Sahih Bukhari 1773)
"Umrah in Ramadan is equivalent to Hajj." (Sahih Bukhari 1782)

CHAPTER 4: EID AL-ADHA AND SACRIFICE

"It is not their meat nor their blood that reaches Allah; it is your piety that reaches Him." (Quran 22:37)

Rules of Qurbani (Sacrifice):
- Obligatory (Wajib per Hanafi; Sunnah Mu'akkadah per majority)
- Must be from eligible animals: sheep (1 year), goat (1 year), cow (2 years, shared up to 7), camel (5 years, shared up to 7)
- Performed on 10th, 11th, or 12th Dhul-Hijjah
- Distribution: 1/3 for family, 1/3 for relatives/friends, 1/3 for the poor
- The one sacrificing should not cut hair or nails from 1st Dhul-Hijjah until the sacrifice
